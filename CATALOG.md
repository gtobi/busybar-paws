Paws is a cat that lives on your BUSY Bar and does well when you treat yourself well.

It works beside you through a focus session, glasses on, and takes its breaks with you. It asks for a break when you skip them, for water through the day and for lunch at noon, and it goes to sleep when your working day is over. Nothing keeps score against you: a request left unanswered changes Paws' picture and nothing else.

## What it does

- **Focus sessions** with the minutes left large on the front display, a goal for the day and a long break after every fourth.
- **Breaks** of eight kinds, picked with the wheel: rest, read, yoga, a cup, a stretch, a walk, a workout, and a smoke break for those who switch it on.
- **Water and lunch**: a goal of glasses for the day, asked for at even gaps, and lunch from noon until you eat.
- **Look away**: every twenty minutes of a session Paws looks out of a window for twenty seconds. So should you.
- **In a call**: hold OK and nothing is asked for half an hour.
- **On holiday**: hold Start, and the front display tells the room when you are back.
- **The day and the weeks**: when the day is over the back display sums it up, with four weeks of dots and your streak.
- **A cat with a life of its own**: twenty things it does at home, another every minute. It chases a laser dot onto the clock, wears a hat on a few days of the year, and has a voice of its own.

## Controls

- **Start**: a focus session, and in one, the break. When Paws asks for lunch or a break, that.
- **OK**: a glass of water.
- **Wheel**: the length of the next session, "not now" when Paws asks for something, the kind of break in a break.
- **Back**: cancels what runs. At home it leaves the app.
- **Hold OK**: a call. **Hold Start**: a holiday.

The front display faces the room and shows one thing at a time. The back display faces you: Paws again, larger, with the day's counts and what each control does now.

Every time, goal and switch is a setting: the working day, the glasses, lunch, the breaks, weekends, the sound.

Paws sends nothing anywhere. What it counts stays on your BUSY Bar.
